Packing and shipment

Veneer is assembled into protected bundles or export crates, strapped and prepared for container loading. Final pack dimensions, sheet count, net/gross weight, moisture barrier and labelling are set in the order specification.

Minimum order quantity: one 40 ft container. Supply basis: FCA or CIF. See the reserved container stuffing schemes page for the future loading-library structure.
