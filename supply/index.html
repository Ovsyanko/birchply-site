Commercial framework

Supply terms

A clear default basis for enquiries, followed by product-specific confirmation in each offer and contract.

Incoterms

FCA named mill, warehouse or terminal; or CIF named port. Exact place, port and Incoterms® edition are stated in the offer.

Payment

Default term: advance payment. Available contract currencies: USD, EUR, RUB and CNY, subject to agreement and a workable banking route.

Minimum order

One 40 ft container per shipment. Mixed specifications may be considered only where mill packing and loading constraints allow.

Origins

Supply can be arranged from Russia, Kazakhstan or Turkey. Product availability, banking route, logistics and documentation are evaluated for each destination and shipment date.

How an offer is built

StageOutput
1. RequirementProduct, end use, format, thickness, grade, quantity, destination and required documents.
2. Mill matchingAvailable origin, production programme, lead time and sample basis.
3. Commercial offerUnit basis, Incoterm, currency, payment, validity, packing and planned shipment window.
4. Specification lockSigned technical specification, grade references and inspection method before production or allocation.
5. ShipmentLoading record and the agreed commercial, transport, origin and quality documents.

Document set

The final set is destination- and product-specific. It can include commercial invoice, packing list, transport document, certificate of origin, quality certificate, inspection report and any contractually agreed certification evidence.

The certification page is reserved for verified supplier certificates and validity data.

Container loading

Pack sizes, gross weight, container payload, axle and floor limits, dunnage and securing are checked against the selected product. Detailed diagrams will be published on the container stuffing schemes page.
